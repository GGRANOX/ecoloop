What is ECOLOOP

One photo. Four AI techniques. One right bin.

Wrongly sorted waste contaminates whole batches of recyclables. ECOLOOP recognises the material from a picture, weighs the evidence with probability theory, decides whether it is confident enough to act, and plans how a sorting robot reaches the correct container.

The decision pipeline

How the AI decides

STEP 01 · VISION

CNN classifier

MobileNetV2 (transfer learning) looks at the photo and outputs P(plastic) and P(paper).

STEP 02 · BAYES

Evidence update

Prior knowledge × likelihood of the CNN's evidence → posterior probability of each material.

STEP 03 · MDP

Decide the action

Value iteration compares Sort Plastic, Sort Paper and Inspect Again by expected reward.

STEP 04 · A*

Plan the route

The robot finds the shortest obstacle-free path to the chosen container.
